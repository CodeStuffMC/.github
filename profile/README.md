# 🧩 Coder Addon Registry

### **Extend the ecosystem.**

The **Coder Addon Registry** is the addon hub for the CodeStuff ecosystem.

Created by **FireSmasher**, the registry provides a dedicated place where developers can discover addons and share extensions that expand what their projects can do.

### 📦 Discover

Explore addons created for the ecosystem and find new ways to extend your development environment.

### 🛠️ Create

Build your own addon and turn your ideas into extensions that others can use.

### 🌎 Share

Publish your creations and contribute to the growing CodeStuff developer ecosystem.

### 🧩 One ecosystem. More possibilities.
